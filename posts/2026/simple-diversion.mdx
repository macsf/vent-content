About a month ago, I learned about this new **microcontroller** called ESP32. It's a low-power System on Chip (SoC) that has integrated WiFi, Bluetooth, and a relatively good processor. At first, I can't think of any use for it. And since I already have a Raspberry Pi that I only use to run internal networking, I thought this ESP32 would be just another gadget that would lie around doing nothing.

Then I keep seeing people playing with this ESP32-thing, doing various interesting projects. And I keep seeing the board+screen combinations, very low price, on Shopee.

Decided to get myself one.

![Such a beautiful simple board](https://macsfvent.sgp1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/media/2026/10/esp32_01.webp)

Admittedly, this is quite an impressive piece of hardware. Such a simple board, yet it has so many potentials. I read posts from people who use it to build simple dashboard, personal assistant device, all the way to a desk buddy that connects to AI models.

But I started with a clock. 😅

![ESP32 Clock](https://macsfvent.sgp1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/media/2026/10/esp32_02.webp)

Then I left it for almost two months until just a couple of days ago.

I sat at home working on some client document, very tedious, very boring. I looked at this ESP32 thing I have around and thought "Let AI has a go at it".

First, I tell the AI to make a code for a clock that shows the current local time and 2 more time zones. And I let it run while I continue tediously working on the document.

![AI made this clock](https://macsfvent.sgp1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/media/2026/10/esp32_03.webp)

Not bad at all.

Next morning before going out for a meeting with the client, I wrote down some idea for what I want to have on the screen beside the clock (if it only runs as a clock, it is a very expensive clock). So I thought ...

1. Pomodoro timer (got an idea from somewhere)
2. Weather forecast
3. Exchange rates

I then told the AI to start thinking about how to where to get these information, and how to display them on the screen. I gave it some drawings for what I want the screen to look like, and I let the AI work on it while I get ready to go out.

I'm sure the AI finished before I finished getting ready. 🤣

There are changes here and there that I told the AI to adjust then I went out.

But not forgetting to ask the AI to think about **how the user can make changes to the display?** If the user want to change the currency, add or change countries for the time zones, or whatever. I told the AI to think about the web interface.

Long story short, the AI built me a personal dashboard with configurable web interface by the time I got to the client's office.

![The screens](https://macsfvent.sgp1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/media/2026/10/esp32_04.webp)

Of course, that's not the end of the story. Sitting in the meeting room waiting for the clients, I thought, "What if I want to see this dashboard on my phone?"

Yep, told the AI to **adjust** the output for using on the phone or desktop.

![Dashboard](https://macsfvent.sgp1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/media/2026/10/esp32_05.webp)

Quite good, I would say.

Of course all these are not done in one iteration. But it only took a day to finish this project.

AI can be good but as always, don't trust it blindly. Always double check.

And.. yes, it's AI, Artificial Intelligence. Saying otherwise is **not intelligence**. 🎃
